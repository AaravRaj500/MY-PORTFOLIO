Who I Am

I'm Arav Raj — a CS engineering student driven by the convergence of autonomous systems, cybersecurity, and intelligent data pipelines.

My work spans writing ROS2 nodes for LiDAR-based rover navigation, building entropy-driven cryptographic tools, and crafting Python anomaly detectors that flag real threats inside server log streams.

I completed Harvard CS50 and IIT Kanpur's Gen AI & Cybersecurity programs, and I'm currently in Year 1 of IIT Madras's BS in Data Science — alongside Harvard CS50 Cybersecurity.

End goal: mastery at the intersection of Autonomous Systems & Security — every machine I build will be as secure as it is intelligent.
